## Phase 5: Decide and fix, one PR at a time (🧑 then 🤖)

This phase runs as a loop: pick an item, agree the fix, open a PR, review and merge, repeat. Each PR applies its fix and re-proves what the fix unblocks, including pass 1's held-back and conditional proofs. Any error found in the thesis goes in [`thesis-corrections.md`](../thesis/thesis-corrections.md) in the same PR. From step 18 on, each PR also refactors as it goes: it moves language-generic content into `lang/` wherever it can and simplifies the protocol proofs with it, while the statement of `safety` stays the same. The order below is a suggestion, not a rule: it separates the language from the protocol first, then makes `safety` meaningful as soon as possible.
